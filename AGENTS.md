## Asset ownership

- Generate the user's character, walking frames, meal, buildings and distinctive photographed objects from their supplied inputs. Never substitute fixed London/demo artwork for these subjects, including during interactions or retries.
- Fixed assets may supply generic terrain, street furniture, transport and identity-independent effects. The shared production interaction catalogue contains effects only; carried food references that scene's generated `food.png`.
- Fixed meals and character poses belong only to explicitly labelled offline/demo fixtures. Keep their catalogue separate from production planning. See `docs/INTERACTION-INTEGRATION.md`.
