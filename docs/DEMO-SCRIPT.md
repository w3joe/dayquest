# DayQuest demo script

Read this cold. Sell the feeling: you can hear about someone’s day and still not *see* it. Codex and OpenAI show up only where they make that feeling possible.

**On screen:** `/demo` (Lucas’s Saturday already in) → optional generating log → a saved `/day/<id>` → Share.

**Voice:** slow. Look at the judges, not the laptop. Pause on the sprite, the ramen on the table, and the link.

Name Codex, gpt-image, and “the model that read the photos” once each. Do not list model versions. Do not say pipeline, Phaser, Blob, structured output, Pokémon, or Emerald.

---

## 90 seconds (video / strict)

About 180 words. Stage directions in brackets. Speak the rest.

### 0:00–0:12 — the ache

*[Black, or a still of the ramen / the bridge. Cut to `/demo`.]*

When someone you love is in another city, you get the photos. You get “how was your day.” You still don’t get to be there.

### 0:12–0:32 — this was a real Saturday

*[Let the map ticks and chips land. Do not click around.]*

This is Lucas’s Saturday in London. The photos he already took. The route he actually walked. The 24 bus. The tube. Five thousand steps in the park.

We used Codex to build this in a hackathon — not a game to beat. A postcard you can walk through.

### 0:32–0:48 — he becomes someone you can follow

*[Hold on the pixel sprite from the fit check.]*

gpt-image looked at his outfit and drew him from behind. Same jacket. Same hair. So when you open the link, you are not watching a stranger. You are walking as him.

### 0:48–1:20 — visit the day

*[Play. Walk to a landmark. Space to read. Exit. Bus or tube if it fits; skip if tight. Ramen on the table.]*

The model read the photos and kept only what he’d tell you. Big Ben from the bridge. Tonkotsu and gyoza at the table. Then a walk in the park.

### 1:20–1:30 — the gift

*[Share. Show the URL. Sit in the silence after.]*

Then you send a link. No account. Their day becomes a place you can visit.

That’s what we wanted Codex for. Not a more advanced demo. A way to miss someone a little less.
