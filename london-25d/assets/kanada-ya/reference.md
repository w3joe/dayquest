# Kanada-Ya, St Giles High Street — reference brief

Photos were looked at as reference only; none are downloaded or shipped.

## Where things really are

OpenStreetMap (Overpass query around 51.5155, -0.1286; https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=19/51.5155/-0.1280):

- **Kanada-Ya**, 64 St Giles High Street (way at 51.51550, -0.12758). **South side** of the street, on the corner of New Compton Street (Chris Pople's review says "on the corner of New Compton St": https://chrispople.substack.com/p/kanada-ya-covent-garden).
- Next door to the west: **The Convenience Store** (63) and **The Angel** pub (61–62 St Giles High Street, Samuel Smith's; https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Angel,_Covent_Garden,_2025-09-16.jpg).
- Then **St Giles-in-the-Fields** church (60 St Giles High Street) in its railed churchyard, also on the south side.
- Further west the street bends north to **Denmark Street** (south/west side) and **Centre Point** / St Giles Circus / Tottenham Court Road station at the NW end.
- North side opposite: **Central Saint Giles** (Renzo Piano, bright orange/lime/yellow glazed facades), Ippudo at 1 St Giles High Street, bus stops.

So in the game the camera stands on the north pavement looking south at the south-side frontages. Facing south, west is on the right, which gives left → right: **Kanada-Ya, The Angel, the church, Denmark Street (guitar shop), Centre Point towering behind on the right**. The tube entrance (Tottenham Court Road) goes on the bottom (north) side towards the right, near Centre Point, where the real entrances are (St Giles Square / Andrew Borde St).

## Kanada-Ya shopfront (St Giles)

From exterior photos (EatClub listing photo via Bing Images, Tripadvisor / Yelp "Kanada-Ya St Giles" photos showing the New Compton St name plate; https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/ search "Kanada-Ya St Giles"):

- Ground floor clad in **pale off-white / blush painted horizontal timber boards**, corner site.
- **Black canvas awning** across the front with **KANADA-YA** in big white letters.
- A **round red logo badge** on the boarding beside the awning (the real logo has a cartoon face; we draw a plain red badge, not the trademark).
- **Tall glazed folding doors** with thin white frames; warm interior with **round paper globe pendant lights**.
- Small grey **hanging blade sign** at the corner (金田家 / KANADA-YA).
- **Wooden benches** along the pavement in front (people sit/queue there).
- Upper floors: **red brick** with stone/white window surrounds.

Not to be confused with the **black** Kanada-Ya frontage on the restaurant's own "Story" page, which is a different branch.

Artistic licence: the building is drawn 2 storeys (it is taller) to fit the 150px shop scale; the spec's small outdoor `table` with the ramen bowl replaces/joins the real benches; the queue of three people is generated (queues are well documented, see below, but the figures are invented).

- **Central Saint Giles** colourful facades on the north side: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:St_Giles_High_Street_January_2012.jpg (not drawn — it would sit on the viewer side).
