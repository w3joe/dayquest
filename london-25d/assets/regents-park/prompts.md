## Notes

Redos and derived tiles:
- triton: the first call (a muscular merman with two mermaids) was refused by the image safety filter at the output stage
  (moderation_blocked, category "sexual"; log kept in logs/triton_v1_blocked.log). Reworded as a bearded sea god with a fish

  tile_water and its lawn noisier than the shared grass, so derive_tiles.py composes the three bank tiles (edge, vertical
  edge, corner) from tile_water + shared tile_grass + that stone band. Run it after tools/process.py.
