# Westminster: reference brief

Scene 1 of the day (SPEC.md day table: 10:00–12:00, "Touring Westminster"; textbox: walked Westminster Bridge, rode the London Eye, photo of Big Ben). The view is from the South Bank (Queen's Walk, beside the London Eye) looking west across the Thames to the Palace of Westminster. In the game, **up = west**.

Photos were used only as reference. None were downloaded or shipped.

## Shawn's reference photo

From Shawn (his own photo, sent for this scene):

> Taken from the South Bank near the London Eye at dusk, looking west across the Thames. Left to right: the Palace of Westminster's long honey-gold Gothic Revival river front; the tall square Victoria Tower with corner pinnacles and a Union flag; the slender Central Tower spire in the middle; Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben) on the right with gold-lit clock faces, dark slate spire and gilded top. Foreground: Westminster Bridge with green-painted cast-iron arches and crowds walking across. A red double-decker bus at the bridge's far end by the Boudica statue. Far right: Portcullis House with its row of tall black bronze chimney stacks and dark roof, and leafy plane trees along the Embankment. On the river: a white-and-grey commuter catamaran (no brand name) and a pier.

What the photo itself adds:
- The camera stands right beside the London Eye: its white legs and struts fill the bottom-left corner. So the Eye goes on the near (South Bank) side, next to the spawn.
- The Victoria Tower is the biggest mass, left of centre: a square tower with four corner pinnacles and a Union flag. The Central Tower spire stands just right of it. The low river front runs along the whole left two-thirds, with small square turrets at its ends.
- Elizabeth Tower is on the right, where the bridge meets the far bank: honey-gold stone, gold-lit clock faces, dark slate spire, gilded tip. Boudica's dark silhouette stands at the same bridge end.
- Westminster Bridge has low green-painted arches on stone piers, with crowds along the deck.
- Portcullis House is far right: dark slate roof, four tall black chimney stacks visible, plane trees in front of it and between it and Elizabeth Tower.
- A white catamaran with a dark hull sits by a pier along the far embankment, lower right. It carries a brand name; the game sprite has no text or brand.
- Dusk light: a pink-grey sky with a pale yellow horizon and warm-lit stone. The game stays daytime (10:00), with a slightly warm pale-blue sky as a nod to the photo.

Ground surfaces: pale granite paving on both embankments and the bridge pavements; asphalt for Bridge Street, Victoria Embankment and the bridge carriageway; a stone coping and granite wall at the river edge; grass by the Eye (Jubilee Gardens).

Plants: London plane trees along both embankments (shared `tree` sprite).

Things simplified or left out: the South Bank Lion and County Hall at the east end of the bridge, the Embankment's cast-iron "dolphin" (sturgeon) lamps (the shared lamp stands in), Westminster Hall, New Palace Yard and the Abbey (hidden behind the Palace or off-map), most of Parliament Square's twelve statues (one stand-in plinth), the Square's surrounding roads except St Margaret Street, the bridge's seven arches (seen only from the deck here), traffic.
